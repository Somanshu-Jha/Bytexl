The product journey

One document, visible evidence

01

Consent and upload

PDF, image, or text. The dialog explains external AI processing.

02

Extract and check

Values retain units, ranges, and source evidence. Handwriting mode compares two readings; disputed text stays unclear.

03

Understand in either language

Conservative English and Hindi explanations keep the same numbers.

04

Review and save

Correct medicines and results. Acknowledge unresolved OCR after checking the original, then revisit the timeline.
